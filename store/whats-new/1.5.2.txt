- The app is now called HelioFITS Peek in the Applications folder, the Dock and the menu bar.
- Processing records: the record HelioFITS Studio or pyHelioFITS saves beside an exported image now reads in Finder, with a Quick Look preview, a thumbnail of the picture and Spotlight search.
- Open a record to see its report: each layer's data, files and processing steps. Copy it as a methods paragraph, as LaTeX, or as a Python script that opens the same data.
- Error messages and the stamps on exported images and movies say HelioFITS Peek.
